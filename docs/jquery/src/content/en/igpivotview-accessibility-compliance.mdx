## igPivotView Accessibility Compliance

### igPivotView accessibility compliance summary

All Infragistics® Ignite UI for jQuery™ controls and components comply with Section 508, Subpart 1194.22 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973. The [igPivotView accessibility compliance summary chart](#summary-chart) contains the specific rules of Subpart 1194.22 that pertain to the control.

In some cases, to meet the requirements of each accessibility rule, you may need to interact with the control by setting a specific property, but in other cases, the control performs these tasks for you.

<DocsAside type="note">
  As jQuery controls are client-only, consequently some of the rules are unsupported and are marked as limitations.
</DocsAside>
